Official History of the Nation

Follow a communications grad from Dallas from a bank job to a Gen Z media empire, one year at a time.

1997

The Supreme Leader is born

Brittany Alexis Tomlinson, born May 10. Raised in Dallas. Theater kid, improv kid, Tumblr fangirl.

2018

Texas A&M, magna cum laude

Three years, a communications degree, and a Spanish minor. Then a call center, then a bank.

Aug 2019

The first sip

On August 6 she posts 21 seconds of cream soda kombucha. It becomes a permanent reaction meme and one of TikTok's top ten videos of the year.

Sep 2019

Fired, then signed

The bank lets her go on September 4. By year's end she signs with UTA and moves to Los Angeles with Sarah Schauer.

2020

The LA era

A Super Bowl ad, the TikTok that sparked Ratatouille the Musical, and TikTok's New Year's Eve livestream with Lil Yachty.

2021

Host of For You

She hosts TikTok's official podcast, interviewing other creators.

2022

Violating Community Guidelines

A cult-favorite podcast about internet oddities with Sarah Schauer, plus Trixie Motel with Trixie Mattel.

2023

The Report and the Court

The Broski Report launches in May and debuts at number four on Spotify. Royal Court follows in July. In August she wins a Streamy Creator Honor.

2024

The Empire expands

Royal Court season two brings Charli XCX and Saoirse Ronan. The Report covers 48 episodes and declares the Broski Empire.

2025

Singer and honoree

"Adore You," "The Sun," and "Stained" arrive via Atlantic. TIME names her a TIME100 Creator. The Report hits 100 episodes.

2026

The idol takes the throne

Harry Styles sits at her Royal Court in February. In March she co-hosts the Vanity Fair Oscar party livestream.
